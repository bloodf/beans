# Managed policy receipts and replay

See [Protocols](protocols.md#cli--relay) for the account transport and blob protocol.

The relay exposes staged Store primitives for opaque managed policy/roster ciphertext committed together, with frozen version-1 receipt lookup before CAS, checked SQLite FULL and transaction-local Postgres `synchronous_commit=on`, atomic quota/sequence/receipt changes, exact historical retries and separate transaction/blob namespaces. `managed_companion_receipts` survives ordinary roster/blob/group deletion; generic insert/precheck refuses retained blob-ID reuse. History is independently capped at 65536 receipts and 64 MiB charged retained bytes, each receipt at 1024 bytes and combined base64url companion ciphertext at 32768 bytes; true account teardown removes history, replay incarnation and usage with either revoke value. Staged replay starts with empty context/token, through=0 and since=0, captures committed head and ordinary-plus-managed policy linkage in one SQLite read snapshot or Postgres read-only REPEATABLE READ snapshot, and returns one complete policy envelope per page with lookahead. HMAC tokens bind account, durable account incarnation, boot session, cut, expiry, cursor and cumulative bounds; forged/split cuts, changed contexts/cursors, expiry and teardown/reset reject. Tokens expire after 300 seconds and enforce at most 65537 pages, 1048576 scanned policy envelopes and 1 GiB policy ciphertext; page serialization is bounded at 8 MiB. Managed pages verify immutable request/hash/paired-sequence linkage and advance past the paired roster sequence. Ordinary policies remain a distinct opaque subtype and are unslotted/unaddressed/ungrouped; backend deletion predicates preserve policy history. Replay tokens do not establish plaintext consent or client readiness: consumers still validate/decrypt/persist and align latest roster correspondence. These primitives have no registered production route or sync consumer and do not activate protocol 6.
